Optional local AI

On supported devices, Chrome’s built-in model can select everyday examples from Lynk’s reviewed explanations. No extra app, API key, or AI library is needed. It runs only when you ask and cannot change a warning.

Checking support does not download a model. Open “Help me understand” on a checked link for explanations.

Chrome currently requires 22 GB of free disk space plus eligible hardware. Model files may be large and are managed by Chrome. Only fixed risk-code names reach the model; no website text or full URLs. Availability varies by browser, device, and policy.

A useful layer, with limits

No flags does not mean a site is safe. Lynk cannot inspect your wallet’s own confirmation screen, simulate transactions, detect every malicious contract, or revoke approvals. A compromised legitimate site may pass URL checks. Keep wallet security alerts enabled and review every signature.

Lookalike navigation checks can run after a request starts. Page warnings are best effort; hostile pages can interfere with them. Chrome restricts extensions on internal pages, the Chrome Web Store, and other extensions. Incognito requires separate permission.
